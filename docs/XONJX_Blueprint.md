# XONJX 13th Grade App – Technical Specification & Feature Blueprint

This document outlines the vision and planned features for the XONJX 13th Grade App. The platform unifies education, housing, franchise management and Web3 capabilities for the entire XONJX ecosystem.

## 1. 13th Grade Learning Management System
- Age-based curriculum tiers for youth and veterans
- Video courses with quizzes and certifications
- Secure student profiles and learning analytics
- Personal Career Development Plan (PCDP) builder
- "Legacy Curriculum" library and faith-based micro-lessons
- Virtual classroom (Zoom/Teams integration)
- AI-driven mentor matching

## 2. Transitional Housing Module
- Intake applications and room assignments
- Meal plan scheduling and chef inventory
- Agenda tracking for daily activities
- Medical check-ins and incident reporting
- Graduation readiness dashboard
- Post-exit support follow-ups

## 3. Franchise Operations Management
- Franchisee dashboard and onboarding tools
- UCC-1 filing and compliance automation
- Revenue split calculators with invoicing
- Royalty tracking and white-label media kits
- Compliance alerts and license renewal management
- Franchise disclosure document (FDD) library

## 4. Marketplace & Product Sales
- eCommerce for mushroom products, supplements and merchandise
- Transitional housing care pack orders
- Location-based delivery coordination and inventory sync
- Subscription management and affiliate integration

## 5. DeFi & Token Wallet Integration
- Ethereum wallet connection for the Antidote Token
- NFT certificates for course completions
- Token rewards for learning and community work
- Wallet dashboards for franchisees, students, mentors and donors
- Smart-contract royalties and tokenized assets

## 6. Real-Time Dashboard & Analytics
- Revenue and student progress tracking
- Housing activity logs and grant reporting
- Donor impact summaries and audit trails

## 7. Mentorship & HR Module
- Background checks and onboarding workflows
- Mentor scheduling and feedback forms
- Matching algorithms and stipend payments
- Mental health and spiritual support tools

## 8. Communication & Community
- Secure in-app messaging and group channels
- Emergency alerts and virtual chapel/prayer wall
- Event calendar with virtual registration

## 9. Document Generation & Legal Templates
- Donation receipts and compliance letters
- Franchise agreements, housing contracts and UCC-1 filings
- Investor documents and SBA loan-ready plans

## 10. Donor & Investor Portal
- Tax-deductible donation tracking and impact metrics
- Grant allocation summaries and legacy giving tools
- Token wallet access for donors and investors

## Security & Infrastructure
- 2FA and biometric authentication
- HIPAA/FERPA compliant data encryption
- Ethereum-based smart contract logs
- Role-based access control
- Uptime goal of 99.9% via cloud and decentralized services

## Platform Architecture Overview
- **Frontend:** React Native (iOS/Android)
- **Backend:** Node.js or Python with PostgreSQL
- **LMS API:** SCORM/xAPI with Zoom SDK
- **Token Integration:** Web3.js and MetaMask
- **CRM/ERP:** Salesforce, QuickBooks, Stripe, PayPal
- **eSignature:** DocuSign/HelloSign
- **Reporting:** Power BI or Tableau connectors
- **Future:** GPT-powered voice assistant for mentors

## Phased Rollout Plan
**Phase 1 – MVP (Q3 2025)**
- LMS core, franchise dashboard, housing intake, donation receipts

**Phase 2 – Expansion (Q1 2026)**
- Token wallet, mentor matching, UCC-1 contracts, eCommerce

**Phase 3 – National Launch (Q4 2026)**
- Investor reporting, multi-state coordination, DeFi staking, AI mentor tools

## Summary
The XONJX 13th Grade App aims to empower students and veterans while automating franchise and financial operations. It digitizes transitional housing support, manages donors and grants, and enables decentralized ownership through Web3 technology.
